20+ young people sat down with four experts: an elite scout from KV Mechelen, the academy director of Royal Knokke FC, an ex-professional footballer and an elite coach. It was an honest, unfiltered conversation about talent identification, academy pathways and what it really takes to succeed in sport.

**What we covered**

- What scouts look for beyond technique
- The academy pathway
- The pro mindset
- A career roadmap

It was the first step of Sport for Solidarity, co-funded by the European Solidarity Corps, with JINT.
