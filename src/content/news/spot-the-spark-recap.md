In March 2026, more than 20 young people joined **Spot The Spark**, DIALI's talent identification workshop.

Four experts sat with them: an elite scout from KV Mechelen, the academy director of Royal Knokke FC, an ex-professional footballer and an elite coach.

The conversation covered what scouts look for beyond technique, how the academy pathway really works, the pro mindset and how to build a career roadmap.

Spot The Spark was the first step of Sport for Solidarity. The next one is **The Mind Behind the Game** on 20 December.
