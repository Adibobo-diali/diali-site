On Sunday 20 December 2026, DIALI hosts **The Mind Behind the Game**, a youth sport conference on mindset, mental performance and wellbeing, at De Felix in Gentbrugge.

Doors open at 10:00. The programme runs from 10:30 to 13:30, with a break and networking included.

**Registration only, limited seats.** Seats open in three waves: rows B–C from 5 November, up to row E from 19 November and up to row G from 3 December.

The first voice is confirmed: **Stallone Limbombe**. More voices will be announced.

Straight after the conference, the **FC 27 tournament** puts the mind under pressure: 16 players, knockout, one winner.

The conference is part of Sport for Solidarity, co-funded by the European Solidarity Corps, with JINT.
